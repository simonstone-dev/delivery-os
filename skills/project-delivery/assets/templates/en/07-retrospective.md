## Actuals and variance

Compare estimates with actual labor, rework, waiting, external cost, acceptance and value. Separate scope changes from estimation errors. Planned effort is not historical actual effort.

## Reusable learning

Preserve evidence, distinguish proven causes from guesses, state limits and propose a minimal method change. Remove client identifiers before sharing. Validate whether the change reduces omissions or rework on the next project.
