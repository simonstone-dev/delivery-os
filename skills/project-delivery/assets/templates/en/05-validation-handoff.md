# Validation and handoff

Working template; no project facts have been established.

## Experiment and acceptance records

Record inputs, versions, method, raw output, observations, limits and approvals. Prototype, technical pass, business acceptance and production approval are separate states. Historical results apply only to recorded conditions.

## Business verification

Choose appropriate replay, held-out samples, pilot or shadow operation. Measure result quality, automated coverage, total human effort and realized value separately. Do not lower specifications to claim success.

## Go-live and rollback

Freeze the release, configuration, data ownership, cutover, migration checks, backup/restore, rollback triggers and responsible people. Confirm who approves production use.

## Handoff and closeout

Check agreed source code, deployment package, data dictionary, interfaces, component licenses, test evidence, manuals, training, accounts and support. Track remaining obligations, acceptance, payments and service exit separately.
