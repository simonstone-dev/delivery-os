# Implementation and commercial plan

Working template; no project facts have been established.

## Scope and basis

State scope version, estimate date, purpose, unit, exclusions and assumptions. Follow the user's request when deciding whether to include money.

## Work packages and AI

Estimate optimistic, likely and pessimistic effort per deliverable, role and dependency. Include discovery, data, integration, QA, security, management, deployment, training and support. Measure AI savings across the whole accepted task.

## People and calendar

Cross-check estimated work against actual role availability. Account for approvals, customer responses, critical path and business seasons. Capacity is not proof of required effort.

## Costs, price and terms

Separate labor cost, external one-off and recurring costs, risk and selling price. Missing prices remain unknown. Explain currency, tax basis, validity, payment triggers, responsibilities, support and change pricing. Avoid double counting.
