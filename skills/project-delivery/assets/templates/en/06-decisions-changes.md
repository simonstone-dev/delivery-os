# Questions and change control

Working template; no project facts have been established.

## Open questions

Explain the source, impact, owner, needed information and next action. Investigate what the team can resolve; ask clients for private samples, permissions and business decisions. Suggested contacts are not confirmed departments.

## Decisions and changes

Record options, evidence, decision authority, date, scope and superseded version. Analyze affected requirements, architecture, tasks, validation, effort, price and milestones before changing commitments.

## Current state and history

Keep current state concise and internally consistent; preserve older records as history. Record real long-running job IDs and the next safe action. Updating records does not authorize external communication or deployment.
