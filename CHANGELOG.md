# Changelog / 版本记录

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-20

First public release candidate. English and Chinese documentation, language-matched portable projects, synthetic walkthroughs, strict record checks, source-file verification, handover-state checks, effort aggregation and canonical report generation.

首个公开发布候选版。提供中英双语说明、对应语言的独立项目、合成演示、严格台账检查、来源文件核验、移交状态检查、人天汇总和依据台账生成的报告。

Adds cross-platform CI configuration and reproducible release checks. Derived from an earlier local prototype; no private case material or machine-specific verification logs are distributed. Full real-project delivery and universal AI productivity gains are not established by this release.

加入跨平台CI配置和可复现发布检查。源于早期本地原型，未打包私有案例与本机日志；本版本不据此声称已验证完整真实项目或统一AI提效。
